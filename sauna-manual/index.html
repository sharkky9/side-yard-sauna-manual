Do not skip the gates

This is a serious DIY guide, not stamped construction drawings. Before buying everything, confirm local permit rules, property setbacks, height limits, electrical requirements, heater listing requirements, and pier depth. Use a licensed electrician for the sauna circuit and final heater/controller installation.

The structure should be boring

Poured piers, standoff bases, short posts, a stiff platform, structural sheathing, a dry roof, and simple serviceable details. The sauna experience comes from the bench geometry, heater, air path, and clean interior materials.

Site fit gate

The side yard decides what is allowed.

The sauna can be compact and handsome, but the revised sidecar version is wider than the pad. Before you buy the shell, mark the as-built pad, the fence line, the house/service side, the electrical panel clearance, the door swing, the landing, and the roof water path.

Do not proceed until

  • Utility locate is complete before digging.
  • Electrical panel working clearance is physically marked and preserved.
  • Door swing and a non-slip landing fit without stepping into loose DG.
  • Roof drip edge, gutter, or discharge path will not soak the fence, panel side, or pier bases.
  • Sidecar version has an as-built pad survey; its 150 in x 98 in platform extends beyond the DG pad sideways.

Property-quality rules

  • Keep conduit neat, direct, and serviceable.
  • Use one roof plane and one siding/trim language if the sidecar is built.
  • Use shielded warm exterior light, not a bright utility flood.
  • Keep storage away from fuel, solvents, pesticides, propane, paint, and battery chargers.
  • Leave enough access to inspect the fence side, roof edge, vents, pest screens, and siding bottoms.

Hot-room performance contract

Loyly is designed before the walls close.

This is a sitting sauna. The comfort target is two excellent upper-bench seats, a real third seat turned onto the short return, and a fourth only when someone accepts the compromised no-footrest position.

Bench geometry

  • Finished ceiling target: 94-96 in.
  • Upper bench: 50-52 in above finished floor.
  • Foot platform: 34-36 in above finished floor.
  • Lower step: 16-18 in above finished floor.
  • Top bench to ceiling: 44-46 in, inside HUUM's 44-51 in guidance.

Heater sizing

  • Room volume is about 327 ft3 before glass adjustment.
  • Glass door adds effective volume, so HIVE Mini 6 is now below the practical target.
  • HIVE Mini 9 is the default: 283-460 ft3 range at 240 V.
  • Keep the 28 x 28 in heater planning zone until current clearances are confirmed.

Vent schedule

  • Target six air changes per hour.
  • Supply: adjustable, 50-100 mm diameter, near the heater.
  • Exhaust: opposite side, 100-200 mm diameter, low near/under bench.
  • Drying vent: high on the opposite wall, closed during bathing, open after use.
  • All vent sleeves need flashing, foil integration, dampers, and pest screens before cladding.

Commissioning test

  • Heat-up should be around one hour in a properly insulated room.
  • Log upper-bench head-height temperature and lower-zone temperature.
  • Start water only when stones are fully hot and water evaporates cleanly.
  • Start with about 80 ml ladles, then wait a few minutes before repeating.

Field rules

  • Buy the heater and door from real specs. Buy finish wood from real field dimensions.
  • Keep pressure-treated lumber structural and hidden. Keep hot-room contact surfaces sauna-safe.
  • Use manufacturer connector fasteners in connectors. Generic screws are not the same thing.
  • Photograph every concealed layer: pier layout, platform blocking, wall blocking, wiring, foil seams.
  • Stop after each dry-in layer and ask: if water gets here, where does it go?
  • Mock up the heater, door trim, and bench return before cutting the final bench slats.

Appendix

Details that keep the build from getting weird later.

Electrical handoff

Plan for the HUUM HIVE Mini 9, UKU control system, heat-resistant power cable, sensor wiring, weather-rated equipment, grounding/bonding, disconnect, and a clean conduit path from the nearby panel. The electrician and AHJ must resolve breaker, GFCI/RCD, conductor, raceway, disconnect, and permit choices. Do not use generic relays or bypass sauna safety controls.

Ventilation

Use an adjustable intake near the heater and a low exhaust under or near the bench on the opposite side. Add a high drying vent that opens after use and stays closed during bathing. The door gap helps, but it is not the whole air plan. Vent sleeves belong in framing, foil, flashing, and pest-screen details.

Water and cleaning

Build a solid, cleanable, heat-tolerant floor under removable duckboards. Give water a deliberate place to go, turn waterproofing up behind the wall base, and keep the wall perimeter inspectable.

Fastener rules

Use stainless fasteners inside the hot room. Use hot-dip galvanized or Simpson-approved connector fasteners outside and anywhere pressure-treated lumber is involved. Do not use drywall screws.
